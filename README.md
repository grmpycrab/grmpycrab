## About

I build software, design interfaces, and record music, sometimes all three in the same week. Most of what I make starts as a question I want answered, not a spec I was handed.

I'm based in Mati, Davao Oriental, Philippines.

<br>

## Build

Software, systems, and small experiments — built with curiosity and shipped when they're ready, not before.

**[TrekScan+](https://github.com/grmpycrab/TrekScanPlus)**
A digital trekking companion for the Mt. Hamiguitan Range Wildlife Sanctuary, a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Davao Oriental. QR-based navigation, geofencing, and offline support for a trail with unreliable signal.
`Flutter` · `Firebase` · `GPS` · `Geofencing` · `Offline-first`

**[Harmonica](https://github.com/grmpycrab/Harmonica-v2)**
A music learning app built for producers — bridging the software I write and the music I make.
`Flutter` · `Riverpod` · `Music theory`

<br>

## Create

Technology is one of my mediums. So are sound, visuals, and design.

Outside of code, I write, produce, record, and mix music as **Keynt Harly**, made independently from writing to final master. I also design brand identities, event materials, and course visuals, usually leaning toward clean type and restrained color.
